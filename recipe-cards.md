## 1. Vegetable Teriyaki Noodles
My Ginger Garlic Kitchen · ~20 min · serves 4  
https://youtu.be/R4SqKmbsiZY

**Remember**
- Cook noodles first. Oil them so they don’t glue.
- Veg stays crunchy. Don’t stew them.
- Sauce last. 50g bottled teriyaki is the whole sauce.

**Do**
1. Boil noodles. Drain. Rinse cold. Toss with 2 tsp oil.
2. Hot pan, 2 tbsp oil, garlic 1 min.
3. Add sliced onion, carrot, peppers, mushrooms, broccoli + pepper + chili flake. Stir until just tender.
4. 50g teriyaki. Coat. Add noodles. Toss. Salt + optional hot sauce.
5. Sesame. Peanuts only if you want them.

**Watch**
- Soft veg = cafeteria noodles.
- Salt before tasting the teriyaki and you’ll oversalt.
- Peanuts are garnish, not required.

Taste 6 · Nutrition 7 · Unique 3  
Fine weeknight bowl. Nobody talks about it the next day.

## 2. One-Pan Mongolian Beef Noodles
RecipeTin Eats · 15 min · serves 4  
https://youtu.be/k9X6K6nvrbY

**Remember**
- Instant noodle cakes, uncooked. Throw the seasoning packet.
- Sauce first, water second, noodles last.
- Broccoli small or it stays raw.

**Do**
Mix sauce: 2 tbsp dark soy, 3 tbsp hoisin, 2 tbsp mirin (or Shaoxing + 2 tsp sugar), ¼ tsp five-spice, 2 tsp sesame oil.

1. Hot deep pan, 2 tbsp oil. Onion + 2 garlic, 30 sec.
2. 500g beef mince. Brown. Pour in sauce. Coat.
3. 5 cups small broccoli. Stir.
4. 2¼ cups water. Simmer. Break 4 noodle cakes in half. Push in.
5. 30 sec, flip, 30 sec, then untangle. 2–3 min until sauce is gone.

**Watch**
- Boiling the noodles separately ruins the point.
- Big broccoli florets stay hard.
- Light soy works but the color goes pale.

Taste 8 · Nutrition 6 · Unique 5

## 4. Crispy Salt & Pepper Chicken Thighs
Xiao Li’s Kitchen · ~50 min with marinade · 500g  
https://youtu.be/J_croDE-LtI

**Remember**
- Beer soak 10 min. Don’t rinse after.
- Cornstarch + oil seal before the pan.
- Skin down. Don’t touch for 3–4 min.

**Do**
1. Boneless skin-on thighs. Tap with knife back.
2. Cover with 330ml beer. 10 min. Drip off. No rinse.
3. Marinate 30 min: ginger, scallion whites, ½ onion, 1 tbsp light soy, 1 tbsp oyster, 1 tsp 椒盐 / salt-and-pepper mix.
4. Dump the aromatics. Toss chicken with 1 tbsp cornstarch + 1 tbsp oil.
5. Medium-low pan, 2 tbsp oil. Skin down. 3–4 min still. Flip. Cook through.

No 椒盐? 1 tsp salt + ½ tsp pepper + pinch five-spice.

**Watch**
- Moving the skin early tears it.
- High heat burns the coat before the meat is done.
- Rinsing after beer washes off the tenderizing.

Taste 8 · Nutrition 6 · Unique 7

## 5. One-Pan Chicken Alfredo
Steel Pan Guy · ~20 min  
https://youtu.be/BatIAXoxKhk  
American fond Alfredo. Not Roman.

**Remember**
- Chicken is for the brown bits. Sauce lives on those bits.
- Cream on the fond. Cold butter + cheese off heat.
- Pinch of nutmeg in every white sauce.

**Do**
1. Preheat stainless. Water droplet should dance. Tiny bit of high-smoke oil on the chicken, not a slick in the pan.
2. Salt-and-pepper chicken breast. Sear ~2 min a side. Don’t rip it off early. Rest it out.
3. Pour cream (half-and-half or heavy) onto the fond. Scrape. Pinch nutmeg.
4. Cook fettuccine. Save a splash of pasta water.
5. Off heat: cold butter (~4 tbsp) then handfuls of Parmigiano (Romano if you have it). Toss pasta + sliced chicken. Loosen with pasta water if tight.

**Watch**
- Cheese on a raging burner = grainy sauce.
- No fond = bland cream.
- This is not authentic Alfredo. People in the comments will tell you.

Taste 8 · Nutrition 4 · Unique 4  
7.9 million views. Half the comments are “that’s not Alfredo.” The other half made it for dinner.

## 6. Fried Apple Hand Pies
Well Made by Kiley · ~2 hr 20 min · 11–13 pies
https://youtu.be/r5Tmse4kndw
https://www.wellmadebykiley.com/blog/fried-apple-hand-pies

**Remember**
- Cool the filling all the way. Warm filling leaks.
- Cold dough. Fork the seam, then fold the edge in.
- Oil 360–370°F. Use a thermometer.

**Do**
1. Filling: 58g butter, 4 diced apples, 110g brown sugar, 60g maple, cinnamon, pinch salt. ~10 min. Slurry 11g cornstarch + 1½ tbsp water, 1–2 min. Vanilla off heat. Cool.
2. Dough: 420g flour, 12g sugar, 8g baking powder (blog writes “2”), 6g salt. Cut in 170g cold butter. 7 oz cold half-and-half + 1 cold egg. Disc. Chill 30 min.
3. Roll. Cut ~4½" rounds. Heaping tablespoon filling. Egg-wash the edge. Fold, fork, fold the edge in. Chill.
4. ~2" oil. Fry ~2 min a side. Glaze warm: 120g powdered sugar, 58g browned butter, 80g maple, 1 tbsp milk, vanilla, pinch salt.
5. Oven instead: 400°F, 22–25 min, egg wash, a vent slit.

**Watch**
- More than a heaping tablespoon will not seal.
- Kneading makes a tough crust.
- Once frying, hold about 350–365°F. Low = greasy. High = brown outside, raw inside.

Taste 8 · Nutrition 2 · Unique 5
79k views. People call them a favorite. Fried, not baked, is the reason.

No peanuts. No legumes.

## 9. Smoked Chuck Roast
jackieh · smoke 3 hr + crockpot 3–4 hr · 2–3 lb
https://youtu.be/1GNM2FCi02E
Recipe is the video description. No blog.

**Remember**
- Smoke at 225°F for 3 hours. Flip once.
- Then broth halfway up the meat. Crockpot on high, 3–4 hours. Flip once.
- Store it in some of the liquid or it dries.

**Do**
1. Coat a 2–3 lb boneless chuck with oil and a steak rub. He uses Black & Tan by Fire & Smoke.
2. Smoke 225°F, 3 hours, flip halfway.
3. Crockpot on high. Add 1–2 cups bone broth or beef stock, about halfway up.
4. 3–4 hours, flip halfway.
5. Cut big chunks, then split with a fork. Don’t shred the whole roast into strings.

**Watch**
- A full shred goes stringy.
- A dry container tomorrow means you left the liquid behind.
- Too little broth and it never half-covers.

Taste 8 · Nutrition 6 · Unique 4
2.8 million views. The method is the whole recipe. People make it because the steps are short.

## 12. Egg Drop Soup
Jujumao · stock 1–2 hr · serves unknown
https://youtu.be/tZoscy6Fc0M

**Remember**
- Blanch the chicken and the feet. Throw that water out.
- Gentle simmer, then strain.
- Flick the eggs forward. Do not dump them.

**Do**
1. Blanch 1 kg chicken feet and 1 whole chicken with a thumb of sliced ginger and 2 tbsp Shaoxing, 5–10 min. Discard the liquid. Rinse the scum.
2. Soak 3/4 cup dried mushrooms, and 1/2 cup dried scallops if you want them, 15–30 min.
3. Clean pot: chicken, 3 smashed ginger pieces, 1 cup chopped green onion, 1 medium radish, the mushroom liquid, 1–2 tbsp Shaoxing, water to cover. Simmer 1–2 hr. Strain.
4. Season: fish sauce, 2 tsp white pepper, 1 tsp sugar, 2 tbsp sesame oil, 1/4 cup light soy, salt. Thicken with potato starch. Start at 1/2 cup starch in a little water.
5. Stream in 6 beaten eggs, flicking. Sliced scallions.

**Watch**
- Skip the blanch and the stock stays cloudy.
- Too much slurry and it turns glue.
- Eggs dumped in become clumps, not ribbons.

**Tools** (hide)
- Pot for the blanch, pot for the simmer, strainer, bowl for the eggs and the slurry.

Taste 8 · Nutrition 6 · Unique 7
6.9 million views. People put it on when they feel sick. The stock is the point.

Legume: light soy sauce. No peanuts. No tofu, beans, peas, or lentils. Dried scallops are optional.
